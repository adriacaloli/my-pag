Research Experience

Postdoctoral Fellowship, Arizona State University (ASU; 2024–2025)

Postdoctoral Fellowship, University of North Carolina at Charlotte (UNCC; 2023–2024)

Doctoral Researcher (2016 - 2022)

  • Taxonomic resolution of a cryptic bat species complex and assessment of the covariation between morphological and ecological variables using geometric morphometrics, ecological niche modeling and phylogenies. Elevated four subspecies to species level based on these findings.

Candidature essay: Use of morphological, molecular and niche characters in evolutionary studies and their integration for phylogenetic inferences and species delimitation in bats

Thesis: Diversification of Glossophaga soricina in Mesoamerica.

Master Researcher (2013 – 2015)

  • Evolution of plant defense traits (trichomes and tropane alkaloids) in Datura stramonium across native and introduced ranges. Found plants that have evolved free of specialized herbivores invest up to ten times less in defense traits.

Thesis: Adaptive evolution during the invasion of novel environments: changes in anti-herbivore in Datura stramonium.

Undergrad Researcher (2011-2012)
