about

Mexico City, 1988. I did my undergraduate and graduate studies (bachelor, master's, and doctorate) at UNAM in Mexico.

My research experience includes diverse areas such as didactics and philosophy of science, plant defense evolution, bat taxonomy, and non-humate primate population genomics.

I chose Evolutionary Biology not only because it’s the backbone of explaining the history of life, but also because I firmly believe that the correct understanding (and explaining) of evolution allows us to make more informed decisions on everyday life stuff, and procure social justice.
